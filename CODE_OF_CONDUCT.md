# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SiftSC a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Welcome questions and constructive disagreement.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not people.
- Respect privacy, licenses, benchmark terms, and research integrity.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact channel on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This compact policy is adapted from the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
